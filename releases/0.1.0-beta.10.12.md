# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.10.12

- Fullscreen channels opened from Sports Hub or the Live TV guide now use the space occupied by the left menu. The menu space returns when you go back to browsing.
- Continue Watching saves your progress during playback and defers bulk Home and Android TV launcher updates until playback closes. This reduces background work that can cause playback to hitch on slower TV boxes.
