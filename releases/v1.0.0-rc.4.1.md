# Nuvio ReSeq TV 1.0.0 RC4.1

## Sports Hub

- Highlight a fixture in **All Events** to see its match centre on the right. Press **Right** to focus it, **OK** to change section, and **Up/Down** to scroll. Available sections include scoring plays, team comparisons, leaders, league positions and fixture details.
- Open a basketball game to see graphical **Team Stats** and **Top Performers** beneath the channels while you browse the right-hand panel. Box-score player lines provide points, rebounds and assists when category leaders are unavailable. Other sports pin a summary appropriate to the fixture. Hide Scores continues to conceal results.
- League positions show each team with its immediate neighbours in its own division. Cricket adds competition tables where available, current batters and balls faced during play, and leading innings and bowling figures after the match.
- All Events shows local fixture dates and initially selects the next scheduled start. Overdue games take their place in the timeline while results are pending. Highlighted games request fresh scores, and faster catch-up during browsing yields to playback.

## Playback, tracking and TV setup

- Saved episode pauses survive restart and tracker cleanup. After a combined watch of a split episode, Next Up advances past the completed parts.
- When **Prompt to rate after watching** is on, leaving a movie after it counts as watched can open the rating prompt. Automatic prompts are limited to movies.
- Stalker profiles support optional **Device ID** and **Device ID 2**. Changing an ID starts a fresh session with the portal.
- The updater verifies downloads against the release asset's SHA-256 digest when available.
- An automatically selected VC-1 source that fails at startup can fall back to another eligible source when automatic engine switching is off.
- Home uses your saved hero catalogues on the first load. Android TV devices can use Ethernet without requiring Wi-Fi hardware.

## Updating

Open **Settings → About → Check for updates**, or install the universal TV APK over your existing installation.
