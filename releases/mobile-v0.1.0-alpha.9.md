# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 9

- Home highlights fade into the active theme. Flick Pick is accessible from Home and Search, with its ReSeq artwork, grouped filters and a pinned Pick and play action.
- Title Details shows the clear logo when available, the exact Play or Resume episode, and an Up next card. Episode tiles show stills, watched state and progress. Tap the three dots for watched and rating actions. The Posters switch beside Seasons saves the setting for your profile. Cast and extras has cast portraits and trailer thumbnails.
- Play manually shows source tiles with add-on logos, provider and quality when available. Library shortcuts, saved files, IPTV movies and TV Mix use the active theme and artwork.
- Calendar keeps its date selector visible while the daily agenda scrolls. Wider landscapes place the calendar beside the events. The filter button includes the existing content filters, sport shortcuts and team settings.
- Shared mobile actions, headings and sheets now have visible structure in dark and AMOLED themes.

Install over the previous mobile alpha to keep your settings and downloads.
