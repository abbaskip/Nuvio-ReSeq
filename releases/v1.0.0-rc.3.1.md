# Nuvio ReSeq 1.0.0 RC3.1

## Live TV guide

- The guide uses a narrower typeface, square programme cells and a muted version of your selected theme colour. The channel logo sits beside the programme details, with more room for the description and the preview at the top right.
- **Past Shows Kept** now preserves saved listings across guide refreshes. If a provider drops yesterday’s programmes or sends “No Program Guide Available” in their place, you can still browse the titles already saved within your chosen window. Providers can still correct titles and broadcast times.
- Catch-up updates fill gaps without clearing the saved shows between them. If an earlier refresh erased a listing, it can reappear when the provider supplies it again.

## TV Mix and Advanced Discover

Both screens now use a dark background tinted with your selected theme colour. The tint stays subtle even with a bright accent.

## Updating

From the public RC3 release, open **Settings → About → Check for updates**.
