# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 12

- Home has a **Reorder** button beside Flick Pick. Open it, choose **Custom order**, then use the arrows to move lists and the eye to show or hide them. The same editor is under **Settings → Home and title details → Reorder Home rows**.
- Sports Hub and Calendar match sheets put **REC** and the full channel and provider on one row. REC opens recording duration. The channel name tunes in, and eligible channels also have a replay icon.
- Guide programme sheets use matching icon tiles in three columns and two rows when all six actions are available. The grid adapts to larger accessibility text.
- **Find in Nuvio** searches the installed catalogues. A unique match opens its details. For listings with a matching episode, the play button and highlighted episode refer to that airing, including when a different TVDB display order is selected. For ambiguous titles you can choose a result or edit the search.
