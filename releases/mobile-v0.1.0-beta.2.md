# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile Beta 2

**Chromecast can now play saved downloads and Usenet streams from your phone or tablet.** Connect to a receiver on the same network using the Cast icon in Downloads or the player. ReSeq tries direct playback first and can prepare a compatible MP4 on the phone when needed. Keep the phone on the network while casting. You can lock its screen and continue using the receiver.

- Cast controls open on handoff and collapse to a mini-player. Play, pause, seek and manage the active session from the phone.
- Completed offline watches stay pending separately for each connected tracker. ReSeq retries after reconnecting with the original watch date and the tracker’s episode numbering.
- Downloads retain their original episode identity and verified matches for other loaded TVDB orders. Switching orders can update the displayed episode and play the saved file.
- Continue Watching handles refreshed and missing tracker history more accurately. Simkl’s manual refresh reloads history, and MDBList’s Remove last watch targets the individual play. Trakt is available as a personal ratings source.

Install the arm64 APK on recent Android phones and tablets, including the Galaxy S24 Ultra. The universal APK supports other Android architectures. Both update earlier signed ReSeq Mobile installs.

Cast conversion and controls were tested on an Android emulator. Playback on a physical Chromecast is still unverified.
