# Nuvio ReSeq 1.0.0 RC2

## What’s new

- Open **Calendar** below Sports Hub to see upcoming episodes of shows you’ve watched, cinema, digital rental or purchase and disc dates for saved movies, and live sports. Calendar opens on today in your time zone and remembers your Day, Week or Month view and filters.
- Sports default to **My Sports**. Hold **OK** on **Sports** to manage your follows or include all enabled events. Select a sport icon to narrow the schedule, then select it again to restore your previous filter.
- Multiple broadcasts of the same fixture share one card. Open it to see channels carrying that day’s play. Calendar can also identify guide-only matchups, including WBBL and school sports. Followed fixtures awaiting a broadcaster show **Channel TBC**.
- Test cricket coverage is matched to the day of play, with a 90-minute allowance for early coverage. National cricket team follows work across formats. Events appear in time order, with followed teams highlighted and placed first at the same start time.
- Moving between days in Week view reuses completed fixture checks, including empty days. Pressing Back from a fixture returns you to the date you opened it from.
- **Advanced Discover** now searches TMDB titles by relevance when you enter a title with the default filters and sort.

## Updating

On RC1, open **Settings → About → Check for updates**. RC2 installs over the signed ReSeq app and keeps your profiles and settings. Calendar coverage depends on your TV guide and the schedules available from each provider. Calendar labels currently display in English.
