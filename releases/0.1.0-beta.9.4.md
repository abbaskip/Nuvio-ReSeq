# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.9.4

## Changes

- Fixes the background image-cache expiry crash affecting Home and Sports Hub, and a Sports Hub focus crash when a refreshed row becomes shorter.
- Continue Watching retains verified sequential Next Up and resume cards during metadata refresh. Ordinary Play resolves the current TVDB episode without redirecting a known episode to Details; shuffle, profile and selected-order safeguards remain in place.
- TV Now reads current guide listings independently of fixture downloads, bulk matching and guide imports. It includes current replays, highlights and unconfirmed sports programmes, with LIVE, REPLAY and ON AIR labels based on guide evidence. Confirmed replay airings remain out of All Events.
- Live cricket, snooker, tennis, golf, motorsport and other long sessions match programmes that are still airing even when they began hours earlier. Current live fixtures reuse TV Now's existing guide read as soon as fixture data arrives, without waiting for bulk matching or adding a whole-guide startup scan. Confirmed programme matches show the fixture's live score in TV Now; replays and ambiguous airings stay separate.
- Long bowls sessions retain their channel matches. Lawn bowls, cornhole and ten pin bowling can appear without a provider fixture.
- Cricket displays runs/wickets and overs separately, includes declared innings and shows BY INNINGS instead of BY PERIOD. ESPN's non-batting placeholders are excluded, and teams that have not batted are labelled accordingly.
- Includes the withdrawn 9.3 sports expansion (Test cricket, Supercars, Formula 2/3 and missing provider categories), earlier 9.2 improvements and the measured guide/Sports first-use profile improvements from bbd76cd54, retaining all four profile journeys.
- All Events fetches and caches schedules when another date is opened, with a loading indicator and local-day boundaries mapped to each provider calendar. Today retains the existing startup feed.
- Improves build and release caching while retaining regression, lint, signing, upgrade and packaged-service validation.
- Google Drive syncs personal TVDB keys for matching profiles in a separate private file. Anyone with access to that file can read the keys. TMDB keys and tracker sessions stay on the device.

## Validation and limitations

- Complete automated regression, lint-budget and actual packaged-service/signing checks cover this combined release. Device acceptance evidence is recorded with the release.
- Scores, listings and confirmed-live labels depend on provider and guide availability.
