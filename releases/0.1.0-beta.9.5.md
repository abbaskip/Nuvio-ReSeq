# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.9.5

## Changes

- Fixes entire TVDB-ordered series becoming unmatched when an addon mixes episode ID formats. The Office's metadata now includes a TVDB-prefixed special among IMDb-prefixed episodes; ReSeq uses the explicitly identified series parent for stream requests instead of disabling every episode. This also restores verified Continue Watching playback, including Extended Cut S06E03.
- Preserves the selected TVDB order and verified tracking identities. Unknown, ambiguous series parents remain unresolved.
- Improves NNTP startup by overlapping provider login with NZB retrieval, caching recent NZBs in memory, and selecting stored RAR media without probing every volume upfront. Later volume boundaries are verified as playback reaches them.
- Adds bounded parallel NNTP read-ahead and a memory-budgeted segment cache. Addon connection allowances are honoured up to 12, defaulting to 2 when omitted. The startup summary reports the clamped preferred-provider allowance and whether it came from the addon or the default, without adding hostnames, credentials, URLs or filenames.

## Validation and limitations

- The recorded current Office metadata reproduces the failure before the fix. Regression coverage checks all seven selected orders, IMDb and TMDB series aliases, Next Up, resume, and rejection of unknown mixed parents.
- Publication requires the complete regression suite, lint, permanent signing, upgrade compatibility, native-library and packaged-service checks.
- NNTP startup and throughput still depend on the provider, network and archive layout. Compressed or encrypted RAR playback is not added by this release.
