# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 6

Install the universal APK over your existing ReSeq Mobile alpha. The smaller ARM64 download is available for compatible Android phones and tablets.

- Open **Library → Downloads** to see current transfers, play saved movies and episodes, check their sizes and delete individual files. The Downloads heading on Home opens the same list. Files saved by earlier alphas appear under their original profile.
- Tap **Play** to use a completed download before any provider lookup. Next Episode also checks for a saved copy. **Play manually** puts the local file first and keeps the other sources available. To download a specific version, tap its source and choose **Download this file**.
- On a fresh install, linking your Nuvio account can restore Continue Watching on Home immediately, even if you haven’t visited Tracking settings.
- Includes Alpha 5’s smaller shelf headings, rounded posters with titles and years, grouped settings, live tracker selections, and Drive recovery for TV Mix and per-show Shuffle.
