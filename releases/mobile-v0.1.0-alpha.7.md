# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 7

Sports Hub now groups each fixture in a bordered card with a tinted competition header. Team badges and scores line up across the two sides. On cricket fixtures, overs sit below runs and wickets. If the names and scores need more room, the score moves under the team name. Hide scores also hides overs and match status.

My Sports uses swipeable cards sized for your phone or tablet, and All events uses the available width. Tapping a fixture opens its scorecard, channel matches and existing actions.

Install the universal or ARM64 APK over Alpha 6. The download library and local playback options remain available under **Library → Downloads**.
