# Nuvio ReSeq Beta 5.1

- Fixed completed Formula 1 weekends and other multi-day events being incorrectly marked LIVE. Sports Hub now respects the event provider's status instead of inferring live play from the date range.
- Separated live events from TV guide sports shows in All Events, My Sports summaries and followed league timelines.
- Changed On Air and replay badges to blue, keeping confirmed live event badges red.
- Changed the guide's fullscreen shortcut to hold Back for half a second. Holding Left resumes normal guide navigation. Returning to fullscreen keeps the current channel and playback session.

Install over the existing app to retain your account, playlists and settings.
