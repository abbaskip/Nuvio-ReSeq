# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.10

- **Multiview** opens from the Live TV guide. Play several channels together, choose which pane you hear, or zoom a pane and return to the grid. Available layouts follow the device’s memory, decoder capacity and provider connection limits. Switching apps releases the players, and returning starts the selected channels at the live edge.
- **Sports Hub** uses an indexed guide search and reuses channel matches for fixtures whose inputs are still current. Open games and live fixtures get priority. Guide-sourced shows use their airing times, and the status pill shows the initial feed, matching and TV Now work.
- **Guide and storage** updates yield while you navigate the channel grid. Interrupted playlist imports clean up their staging data on restart. A storage-full guide update keeps the previous listings. Upgrades preserve user-managed On Demand corrections.
- **Continue Watching** restores settled cards on startup, retains partial rewatches and resolves an episode through the same selected TVDB order as Details when you open its card. This includes split episodes such as *The Delivery*. If the source is unavailable, the card opens Details on that episode. Removing the last watch preserves earlier history. Switching between the latest and furthest watched episode now updates the Home card even when the watch history stays the same.
- **Immediate undo** now sticks when you finish an episode and remove its last watch in Details without visiting Home first. A delayed final tracker write no longer brings that completion back.
- **Problem reports** are available in Settings → About and the Live TV, Multiview and Nuvio player menus. Review a cleaned technical report on the TV or scan its QR code to open it on your phone, then email or download it. Reports include playback format, decoder and picture sizing details for display problems, without account names, viewing history or credentials. Nothing is sent automatically. If your phone cannot share an attachment from a local network page, download the text file and attach it to the prepared email.
