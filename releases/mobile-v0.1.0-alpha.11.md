# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 11

- Live TV has an icon-and-label section bar. Back returns to the section you opened Live TV from. The Guide puts the active group, search and sources on one row. Tap the group to switch or manage groups, or the clock above the channel column to browse another time.
- Guide channels show a small station logo above a centred, two-line name. Programmes on air use a dark tint from the current theme. Opening the channel list or guide during playback leaves the video visible through the panel.
- Sports filters use ReSeq's sport icons. Two-team Calendar fixtures show both badges, with team initials when artwork is unavailable.
- Download uses the down-arrow icon. Standard IMDb and TMDB ratings show their logos wherever those ratings are available. Shuffle entries in Continue watching carry a shuffle badge on their artwork.
- In playback settings, the next-episode percentage or time appears directly under the threshold choice.
- Includes the Alpha 10 TV Mix cover correction. Install over your existing mobile alpha to keep your settings and downloads.
