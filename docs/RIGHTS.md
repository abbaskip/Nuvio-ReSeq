# Reporting copyright and other rights concerns

For concerns about code, artwork, documentation, release assets or other material actually distributed by Nuvio ReSeq, [open a rights-concern issue](https://github.com/abbaskip/Nuvio-ReSeq/issues/new?template=rights_concern.yml). Identify the work concerned, the exact repository file/commit, release asset or project-hosted URL, the basis of your concern and the change you request. A public reference showing ownership or permission is helpful where available.

Issues are public. Do not post personal addresses, private contact details, credentials, private playlist URLs or confidential evidence. This is an informal project contact route, not a confidential channel or a formal DMCA procedure. For a copyright complaint to GitHub about material it hosts, use [GitHub's own reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/guide-to-submitting-a-dmca-takedown-notice) and review its disclosure policy before submitting. Contacting this project first is not required by this guidance.

For content hosted solely by an external provider, contact that provider. If the concern also involves something this project distributes, identify that material here so it can be reviewed. The maintainer can review project material but cannot remove content hosted solely by another service.

These reporting options do not claim a legal safe harbour, certification or immunity from liability. Software rights remain governed by [LICENSE](../LICENSE) and applicable third-party licences.
