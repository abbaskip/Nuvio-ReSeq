# Nuvio ReSeq TV 1.0.0 RC 4

## Seek previews

Turn on **Seek Thumbnails** in Playback settings to see a preview as you scrub in ExoPlayer or libmpv. With a configured Seekr key, ReSeq looks for a preview for the movie or episode using ReSeq episode matching. When Seekr has no usable preview, ReSeq can generate frames from supported video sources. Select **Seekr only** to turn off local generation.

Thumbnails are off by default. When enabled, the default generation mode works while paused or seeking. Generation yields while the player buffers. Playback settings also let you control 4K generation and cache size. This release upgrades libmpv to 0.2.2 and updates the thumbnail reader to match its bundled FFmpeg.

## Live TV and watch history

- **Search Nuvio** in the TV guide opens Details when a programme has one clear match. For a series, ReSeq can also select the matching episode. Ambiguous matches open search results.
- IPTV can be included as a source in individual autoplay tiers.
- When marking a title watched, choose now, a previous date, or a date and time.

## Google Drive backup

Automatic sync has its own switch. You can also back up and restore manually. The Drive backup includes your seek preview preferences and configured Seekr, TVDB, TMDB, Gemini, debrid, MDBList and AnimeSkip credentials. Anyone who can read the backup file can read those credentials.

## Updating

On your TV, open **Settings > About > Check for updates**. For a manual install, choose the universal TV APK if you are unsure which architecture your device uses.
