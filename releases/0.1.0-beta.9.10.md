# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.9.10

- **Continue Watching** keeps the verified episode on screen while your tracker reconnects and rebuilds the row from your current history. This fixes shows reverting to “Open series to choose an episode” on startup, including shows using an alternate TVDB episode order.
- **Live TV** stores and reads a shared schedule once for channels carrying the same listings. This reduces the space needed for guide updates. An interrupted save or a full database keeps the previous listings available for the next attempt.
- **Sports Hub** shows live and upcoming fixtures as the schedule arrives, displays its loading status, and adds confirmed channels as they are found. You can open an event while the remaining channel search continues.
