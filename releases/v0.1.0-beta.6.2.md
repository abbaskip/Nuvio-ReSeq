# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.6.2

## Changes

- Restores followed whole leagues, including NBA, in My Sports banners, upcoming games and All Events filtering.
- Refreshes games still saved as scheduled after their expected finish. Scoreboard requests use the game's US date, so afternoon results in Australia are not missed at the US date boundary.
- Restores a selected game's local channel and guide lookup even when the preview is stalled or buffering. Newly finished games can offer guide-backed replay immediately.
- Removes the unreliable external cricket calendar that listed an incorrect November 2026 Ashes season. Cricket events from the guide remain available. Date-only calendar events say that their time is unconfirmed.
- Retains the Sports preview/full-screen improvements, Home/Back menu fix, Stalker fixes and Advanced Discover from the withdrawn beta 6 and 6.1 builds. Personal TMDB credentials remain encrypted and local to the selected TV profile.

## Validation and release status

- Private Onn test build only. Beta 6 and 6.1 remain withdrawn; the public updater stays on beta 5.4 while the combined update is evaluated.
- Full Debug suite: 2,941 tests, zero failures, two existing skips. All 10 sports-feed producer tests pass, including removal of cached incorrect cricket entries. Independent Sports Hub/navigation/updater/source-paging CI passes.
- Signed release build, release vital lint and Play Store compilation pass. All five finished APKs contain the approved backend, tracker and Drive configuration; signing and upgrade compatibility over 6.1 are verified. The exact installed Onn APK generated a fresh pairing code.
- Onn: today's Lakers–Heat result and channel matches appear; upcoming NBA banners work; All Events includes listings after 10am; an Austreams guide replay opens in 1080p. The incorrect Ashes entry is absent.
- Austreams Sky Sports FHD preview and full screen, and a guide-backed FIBA replay: each presented new video frames in all 39 measured intervals over approximately 43 seconds. These short samples cannot guarantee every provider stream is interruption-free. The tested audio was stereo; 4K and true 5.1 are not newly validated here.
- Up from the main Sports banner reaches the preview, OK expands the existing stream, and Back returns directly to Sports. The return was recorded and sampled locally. Back from Sports opens the left menu without exiting.
- The originally affected Stalker provider account remains unverified; protocol regression tests pass.
