# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.9.9

## My Sports

- Open My Sports at Live & Upcoming Events on first entry, with the first event focused. Returning from a game keeps the previous card and position.
- Match Australian domestic cricket state names, abbreviations and team names to guide listings, including NSW v South Australia and Queensland v Tasmania, while preserving Victoria v WA matching.

## Continue Watching and playback progress

- Shows stay in Continue Watching reliably, without flickering. The next episode appears straight away after finishing or unwatching, including away from Home.
- Split and combined episodes in TVDB orders track and complete correctly. Existing tracker behaviour is preserved.
- Progress refreshes on Home and after playback instead of on a timer.
- Live TV releases its connection when Nuvio playback, stream lists or trailers start.

## Search, Home and library

- Improve remote and touch search input, focus, deletion, clear/backspace and restored queries, while retaining IPTV and on-demand search.
- Keep folder and Home hero preferences consistent; improve add-on QR installation focus and library bottom spacing.
- Show tracker-aware library empty states with existing custom filters, and list-picker checkmarks with Home and Details actions retained.
- Include the Lithuanian and Dutch updates and feature-related translations. Feature text without a supplied translation uses an explicit English fallback.

## Player and subtitles

- Resume from the current or saved position when switching sources, including HTTP, NNTP, media servers, debrid/torrent, YouTube and IPTV on demand.
- Optionally try the next playable stream after a failure, within the current source tier and existing order.
- Restore the current playback route after process death, including verified TVDB context and renewed NNTP sessions, while retaining TV Mix ownership.
- Repair eligible VOD HLS initialization with bounded reads, and improve AV1 HDR10+ parsing.
- Refresh eligible expired playback links while retaining ReSeq caching, NNTP session handling and Stalker recovery.
- Improve episode-loading feedback and focus the playing source without reordering the stream panel.
- Add chapter controls for both engines, keeping MPV chapters visible, plus optional chapter titles and improved end-credit post-play timing.
- Add subtitle fonts and appearance controls for both engines, preserve existing libass controls and user-positioned subtitles, and improve letterbox masking.
- Persist media-server sidecar subtitles and provide optional AC-3 optical passthrough; default audio behaviour is unchanged.

## Updates and compatibility

- Keep a downloaded update after cancelling installation so it can be installed again. Request reopening after a successful user-started in-app update. Android may block this background launch; reopen the app manually on affected devices.
- Dismiss the keyboard when entering Ambient mode; translate stream-test connection labels across 27 locales.
- Retry thermal-headroom sampling with a bounded backoff, and retain compatible CloudStream bridge interfaces.

## Release validation

The 9.9 candidate's existing validation and TV trial are retained. These final My Sports changes were included without another regression or device test run at the maintainer's request.
