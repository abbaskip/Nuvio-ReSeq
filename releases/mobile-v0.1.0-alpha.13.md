# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 13

- **Settings → Home and title details → Allow trailers on mobile data** controls trailer playback on this phone or tablet. Turn it off to allow trailers only over Wi-Fi or Ethernet. It starts enabled on both new installs and upgrades.
- The setting covers the Play trailer button, the trailer gallery and Watch next recommendations, including automatic previews. Switching from Wi-Fi to cellular stops the trailer and cancels pending trailer lookups. You can still stream films, episodes and live TV on mobile data.
- **Home → Reorder** keeps a moved list on screen when it reaches the top of a long catalogue.
