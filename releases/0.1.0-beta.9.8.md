# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.9.8

## Episode progress

- Add **Remove partial watch** to an episode's long-press menu on the series Details page when a partial resume is available.
- Clear that episode's partial resume while preserving completed watches, older sessions and other episodes. Selected TVDB orders use verified episode identities.
- Translate the new action and its success/error messages across the existing app locales.

## Sports Hub

- Stop linking cricket fixtures to news bulletins that mention the countries or report on the match. Keep genuine cricket listings, including listings on general channels.
- Bring International Cricket into the main cricket picker and apply consistent international team colours throughout Sports Hub.
- Load Rugby League World Cup fixtures earlier, include known fixtures in All Events and retain the full upcoming row.

## Live TV and IPTV On Demand

- Improve spacing in the guide information panel, retain channel metadata in its compact layout and remove the duplicate channel logo.
- Prefer IPTV on-demand sources matching the TV's language, then rank available sources by resolution, encoding, audio and size using already loaded metadata.

## Validation

Local validation passed 3,539 JVM tests with two opt-in skips, 104 pipeline tests with one platform skip, and 14 sports-feed fixture tests. Full lint passed the released-source regression gate after correcting the International Cricket category expectation and completing the new translations. The signed local candidate was installed over the existing Onn app with its data identity retained and no startup crash detected. Publication is gated on the release workflow's full regressions, lint, permanent signing, upgrade identity, native-library and packaged-service checks.
