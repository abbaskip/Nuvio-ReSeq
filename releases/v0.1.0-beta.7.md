# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.7

## TV Mix

Create your own groups of TV shows for casual, continuous random viewing. Long-press a show, choose **Add to mix**, and create or select a saved mix. Your mixes appear at the bottom of Home with artwork from their shows.

Open a mix to see its included shows and the next random episode. Play uses your existing source-selection and player settings. Each show gets an equal chance, watched episodes stay eligible by default, and immediate episode repeats are avoided when alternatives exist. Mix management includes rename, remove shows, delete, and an unwatched-only option. Mixes are saved per profile and included in ReSeq Drive backup/sync.

With autoplay on, natural completion selects another random episode from the mix. With autoplay off, **Continue mix?** offers **Yes**, **No**, and **Remix**. Back ends the session. Existing episode identity, selected TVDB order, Continue Watching, and tracker/scrobble paths are retained.

## IPTV On Demand and Live TV

- Season/episode matching accepts filenames such as `S01-E03`, including spaced dashes, en/em dashes, dots, and underscores. Multi-episode ranges and contradictory episode identities remain excluded.
- Large Xtream catalogs stream through bounded batches instead of building full-catalog objects in memory. Interrupted responses retain completed batches and existing titles; only a complete refresh removes stale titles.
- Playlist/category recovery and database migration checks cover large imports and interrupted refreshes.
- Guide detail reads and repeated remote-key handling are throttled while preview video is playing, preserving the existing Back-to-fullscreen and group visibility controls.

## Sports Hub

- League logos can appear before fixtures load and update when feed artwork arrives.
- Main competitions precede regional suggestions; related provider entries share one browse card while keeping their original team and fixture identities.
- Existing followed teams and personal row order are retained. Motorsport and horse racing are separate, and Cue Sports includes snooker, pool, and billiards.
- Includes the unpublished standings and guide-display refinements alongside beta 6.3's Sports follow, event-status, and navigation fixes.

## Validation and limitations

This candidate is staged for automated checks and Onn acceptance before publication. Final observed results are recorded in the release description and the staged validation report. External players need to report a natural completion to continue a mix; missing or ambiguous return data ends the session. Source and artwork availability still depend on users' configured providers and metadata services.
