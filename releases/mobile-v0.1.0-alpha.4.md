# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 4

Install the universal APK on a phone or tablet running Android 7 or later. The smaller arm64-v8a APK supports 64-bit ARM devices. Either installs over an earlier signed Mobile alpha and alongside ReSeq TV.

- **Home and Library.** Continue watching includes upcoming episodes, progress, title details, watched controls and the active profile’s presentation settings. Library adds list editing and genre and year filters. Collections support editable folder artwork, TMDB filters and Trakt sorting. Search retains recent queries and loads more results.
- **Series and movies.** Title details use a two-pane layout on larger screens. Add a show to TV Mix from Details and open the mix from Library. The player uses the shared next-episode, post-play recommendation, tracker rating and still-watching settings.
- **Live TV and Sports.** Jump to a date in the guide, manage channel groups and category PINs, tune an upcoming sports fixture from its reminder, record or watch from the start where the provider supports it, and adjust broadcaster matching.
- **Settings.** Manage profiles and PINs, extension repositories and diagnostics, MDBList and AnimeSkip, subtitle styling, player buffers and source fallback. Settings → Home and title details controls highlight sources, upcoming episodes, posters and trailers.
- **Downloads and restore.** Long-press a Home title or Details episode to download, or select the exact source under Play manually. Play checks for a completed local copy first. Connect Google Drive on a new device and choose Restore before making your first backup. Use the same passphrase for an encrypted transfer of additional provider keys. TV RC 3.1 can supply the standard IPTV and TVDB backup.

Downloadable sources must return a video file. Device and provider checks are tracked per screen. [Feature coverage](https://github.com/abbaskip/Nuvio-ReSeq/blob/mobile-v0.1.0-alpha.4/docs/reseq-mobile.md)
