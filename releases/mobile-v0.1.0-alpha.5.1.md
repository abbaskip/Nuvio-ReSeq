# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 5.1

Home now checks which tracker accounts are connected on startup. On a new installation you can sign in to Nuvio and restore Continue Watching immediately, including when you have never opened Tracking settings. A connected tracker you selected remains your source.

This update installs over Alpha 5 and includes its compact poster shelves, release years, chevron navigation, grouped phone and tablet settings, and Google Drive recovery for TV Mix and per-show Shuffle.
