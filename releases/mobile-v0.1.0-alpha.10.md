# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 10

TV Mix covers on Home show the full collage. The mix name appears once underneath, following your poster caption setting. With captions hidden, TalkBack still reads the mix name.

This update includes the artwork-led Details, Flick Pick on Home and Search, source tiles and pinned Calendar introduced in [Alpha 9](https://github.com/abbaskip/Nuvio-ReSeq/releases/tag/mobile-v0.1.0-alpha.9). Install over your existing mobile alpha to keep your library, settings and downloads.
