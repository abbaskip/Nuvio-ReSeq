# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.9.7

## Sports

- Keep ongoing cricket visible through stumps, rain delays and other pauses.
- Keep channels on game pages during detailed lookups, and avoid treating live multi-day games as replays.
- Prioritise the open game and live events when matching guide listings; publish their channels before the wider fixture scan finishes.
- Restore Sports Hub fixtures and matched channels when reopening the app.
- Make Finding channels quieter after the initial matching pass.
- Give the six Australian state teams consistent colours in Sheffield Shield and One-Day Cup, including Women name variants, with readable text on bright bands.

## Live TV playback and guide

- Add **Stop playback** to full-screen Live TV options and Sports preview options. It releases the stream and keeps the preview stopped until a channel or game is chosen.
- **Guide keeps updating (slowed) during playback, with a setting to turn it off.** Updates pause for tuning, buffering, seeking or high memory use, and resume in small batches.

## Large NZB support

- Stream large NZB manifests with memory-aware limits and bounded caching, reducing memory pressure during parsing and playback preparation.

## Update status

- Show playlist, channel and guide update progress once as plain text at the far top-right across the app, including waiting, completion and error messages.
- Keep the indicator hidden during full-screen playback and quiet startup reads.

## Validation

The candidate passed 3,522 JVM tests with two existing skips and no failures, including all 230 Sports and 437 Live TV tests, plus the production minified build and the guide database cancellation/replacement test. In ten-minute fixture comparisons on 2 GB and 3 GB emulators, the guide completed during a sustained Sports preview with no dropped frames or rebuffers; navigation p95 stayed at 17 ms. Signed APKs are built from the versioned candidate commit for bedroom-TV validation before publication.
