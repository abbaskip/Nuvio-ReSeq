# Nuvio ReSeq 1.0.0 RC3

## What’s new

- **FlickPick** picks an unwatched movie from your filters, your saved movies, a connected tracking list or a Home catalog. Choose genres, TMDB keywords, an era and a maximum age rating. FlickPick samples across matching pages and remembers recent suggestions to reduce repeats.
- **Advanced Discover** supports comma-separated title terms and TMDB keywords with **Match any** and **Match every** options. Quotes match a whole word or phrase in a title, or an exact TMDB keyword tag. **Send to FlickPick** uses your current filters to start a movie or a TV episode. TV picks respect resume, episode order and the show’s saved shuffle settings.
- **TV Mix** has a new episode preview with Play and Remix controls. Highlighting a mix on Home now brings up its TV Mix backdrop.
- **Live TV** now opens the full info panel on a **Right** tap, with the first recently watched channel highlighted. Press **OK** to tune, or hold **Right** from live video to switch directly to the previous channel. Right skips forward when watching catch-up or when rewound.
- **Continue Watching** applies the ordinary watched threshold to split episodes so it can advance to the next part even if your Next Episode prompt is set later.
- Upgrading profiles get **Continue Trailer in Details**, **Play in Background** and **Pause When Scrolling** enabled once. You can change these switches in Layout after updating.
- This release incorporates upstream NuvioTV 1.2.0-beta.3, including Plex and Silo support and updates to Home, show details and trailer playback. [Upstream release notes](https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioTV/releases/tag/1.2.0-beta.3)

## Updating

On RC2, open **Settings → About → Check for updates**. RC3 installs over the signed ReSeq app and keeps your profiles and settings.
