# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.10.11

Continue Watching now clears a show after you finish its last available episode, even if earlier episodes have no watch history. If you started tracking a show halfway through, Home clears the old S1E1 suggestion when it refreshes. A later episode can appear when it becomes eligible.

This works with “Up next from furthest episode” on or off. Shows with Shuffle enabled can still offer a random episode.
