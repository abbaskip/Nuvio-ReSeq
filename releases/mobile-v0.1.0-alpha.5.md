# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 5

Install the universal APK on a phone or tablet, or choose the smaller ARM64 APK for a recent Android device. Both upgrade the previous signed mobile alpha in place.

- Home shows compact poster titles and release years when poster labels are enabled. Artwork has gently rounded corners. Tap a shelf heading or its chevron to see all titles. Condensed 14–15sp headings leave room for longer recommendations.
- Continue Watching refreshes the selected tracker on return to the app and after playback. An empty shelf identifies its source and includes a refresh action.
- Settings pages group related controls. Continue Watching, library and ratings source choices update on tap.
- Standard Google Drive Restore includes the TV’s saved mixes and per-show Shuffle choices for matching profiles. On a fresh mobile profile, restored enabled shows make the Shuffle control available.
- For differently named or numbered profiles, open **Settings → Google Drive backup and restore → Restore TV Mix and Shuffle**. Review the saved counts, choose a source and confirm the destination. Other saved mixes and shows stay in place. Home mix artwork now opens the mix.
- Connecting Drive leaves automatic sync off. Use **Restore** for the standard TV backup. Only the separate **Restore keys** action needs the passphrase chosen when creating a provider-key backup.
