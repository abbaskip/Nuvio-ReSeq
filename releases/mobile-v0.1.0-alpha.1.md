# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 1

The first ReSeq release for Android phones and tablets. It installs alongside ReSeq TV as `io.github.abbaskip.nuvioreseq.mobile`.

## Download

Choose the **universal APK** if you are unsure which version to install. The **arm64-v8a APK** is a smaller download for phones and tablets running 64-bit ARM Android. Both require Android 7.0 or later. SHA-256 checksums are attached.

If you previously installed a local debug build of ReSeq Mobile, Android may report a signing conflict. Back up any configuration you want to keep before removing that debug install. The public alpha uses the project's release signing key.

## What you can try

- Add an M3U playlist or Xtream live account and an XMLTV guide. Tap a programme to see its details, play a channel, and switch channels from the player panel.
- Browse Calendar by day, week or month, use its filters and followed-sport shortcuts, or find a fixture in Sports Hub and open a matching channel.
- Filter movies and shows in Advanced Discover, save searches, and build a TV Mix to preview its next episode.
- Connect the existing ReSeq Drive backup service from Setup.

## Alpha coverage

Phone portrait, phone landscape and tablet layouts were exercised with a two-channel playlist and guide on an Android emulator. Physical-device testing and account-backed Drive restore are still pending. Home, Library, on-demand playback, the TVDB order picker, ratings and Usenet controls are planned for later mobile builds. [Feature coverage](https://github.com/abbaskip/Nuvio-ReSeq/blob/mobile-v0.1.0-alpha.1/docs/reseq-mobile.md)
