# Local optimisation measurements

Nuvio-ReSeq dev baseline `3557414`. Measurements used a local NNTP server, synthetic media and an API 34 x86 Android TV emulator. Times are medians unless specified. The emulator shares CPU and disk with the host. These runs can distinguish work on different code paths, but absolute frame and startup times vary with host load.

| Assessment | Fixture and observation | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 · NNTP throughput | 32 × 192 KiB articles, 20 Mbit/s per provider connection. At 50 ms RTT, enabling a second slot raised throughput from 20.1 to 28.4 Mbit/s. At 150 and 300 ms, the guarded version delivered essentially the single-slot rate (19.3 and 12.6 Mbit/s). Unconditional splitting slowed some latency-bound runs. | Use a spare provider slot for read-ahead when measured transfer cost warrants it. Preserve connection limits. |
| C2 · NNTP seek | At 300 ms RTT, reconnecting with queued replies took 1,266 ms at 80 Mbit/s and 1,393 ms at 10 Mbit/s. Adaptive draining took about 363 and 800 ms respectively. At 50 ms/10 Mbit/s, reconnecting was faster than draining. | Choose using measured connection and transfer costs. Bound discarded replies and recover on protocol errors. |
| C3 · Stream screen | A 720-stream, 12-addon emission with existing badges and regex selection took 21.5 ms on Main. Early selection brought it to 9.6 ms. Moving the large list work yielded 10.5 ms elapsed with 0.20 ms median Main CPU (0.27 ms p95). | Offload large emissions. Preserve source order, newest badges and autoplay eligibility. |
| C4 · Sports Hub | A 336-fixture score round with Media3 preview fell from 298 to 94 ms. The preview reported zero dropped frames in both runs. | Reuse normalized team and competition names during each merge. Keep score polling and event matching semantics. |
| C5 · Live guide | Verified D-pad focus moved from channel 1 to 56 in a 200-channel guide. Across two runs, frame medians were 18 and 38 ms, p95s 35 and 63 ms. | Retain guide behaviour. The whole-screen measurement does not identify a particular row or composable to change. |
| C6 · Progress persistence | Four observers, writes with 1,000 and 5,000 records. Times fell from 57.5 and 64.9 ms to 37.2 and 27.2 ms. | Reuse parsed records after checking the actual stored value within the transaction. Skip unchanged writes. |
| C7 · Cold start | Five minified cold launches per run. Main-thread Supabase initialization took 120–426 ms. Sentry settings took 2.8–83.5 ms. Median launch went from 1,274 to 1,135 ms, with overlapping, wide ranges. | Preload the authoritative Sentry setting during initialization and skip it for blank DSNs. The launch timings do not establish a general startup improvement. Supabase auth sequencing is unchanged. |
| C8 · yEnc | Same 200 × 720 KiB articles on the emulator. Block decoding took 748 ms versus 4,338 ms for the dev decoder. Allocations were 152.3 versus 152.5 MB, and GC count fell from 12 to 8. Also ran a 1,055 MiB accelerated fixture. | Keep the block decoder. Article buffer pooling lacks evidence of sufficient benefit. A continuous 30-minute playback run remains unmeasured. |
| C9 · Release logs | During a minute of ordinary playback in a minified release fixture, the player logged six lines, and the platform buffer pool logged twelve. | Retain diagnostics. Hedge-heavy playback was outside this sample. |
| C10 · mpv | Playing and seeking a 64 MiB local MP4 with its index at the end produced three byte-range requests, with two open simultaneously. | Retain mpv caching and allow overlapping readers. A new range alone does not establish abandonment. |
| C11 · Media3 VOD cache | Reading 2 MiB through a Usenet session wrote 2 MiB to disk. Replaying the same URL required zero upstream bytes. A new session URL added another 2 MiB. | Retain same-session caching. Stable cross-session keys require a reliable content identity. |

## Reproducing the fixtures

- For C1 and C2 set `NUVIO_PROFILE_LOOPBACK=true` and run `:app:testFullDebugUnitTest --tests com.nuvio.tv.core.usenet.UsenetPlaybackTest`. The fixture emits its socket, rate and RTT measurements to the JUnit XML.
- For the debug emulator fixtures, assemble `:app:assembleFullDebug` and `:app:assembleFullDebugAndroidTest`. Install the matching app and instrumentation APKs on an isolated emulator. Pass `-e localProfile true` and select `com.nuvio.tv.profiling.GuideScrollProfile,com.nuvio.tv.profiling.LocalOptimisationProfile` with `am instrument -r -w`. Results are emitted as instrumentation status. Specify the emulator serial explicitly.
- C7 and C9 were separate minified-release samples using cold launches, system tracing and PID-scoped logcat during verified playing state. The debug harness does not reproduce those two samples. Account sync and live provider throughput were exercised with test transports rather than personal accounts.
