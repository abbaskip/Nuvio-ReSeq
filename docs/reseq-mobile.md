# ReSeq Mobile

Select the **mobileDebug** build variant in Android Studio or run `./gradlew :app:assembleMobileDebug`. The phone and tablet launcher installs as `io.github.abbaskip.nuvioreseq.mobile`. The existing TV targets are `fullDebug` and `fullRelease`. The two installs have independent Android storage. Drive backup uses the same ReSeq backup format.

## Shared engine and touch layouts

Both apps compile the same Kotlin engine, Hilt graph, ViewModels, repositories, Media3 and MPV players, Usenet and extension runtime. The `mobile` source set supplies the phone and tablet launcher, touch navigation, gesture controls and adaptive screen layouts. Mobile preserves the selected TVDB episode identity through details, source selection, playback, downloads and tracking.

When adding a shared feature, expose its actions from the existing ViewModel and add the touch entry point under `app/src/mobile`. Record the destination below. CI checks the inventory, separate package and launcher contracts, TV unit suite and mobile emulator journeys. To exercise an assembled mobile debug build on a disposable emulator, use `python scripts/mobile_smoke.py --extended --serial emulator-5554`.

**Usenet.** Open a title, choose **Play manually**, then select a result from a configured Usenet add-on. The add-on supplies the NZB and NNTP server details. On the public Alpha 12 APK, a local test add-on served an NZB for a generated film. The built-in player rendered the video, **Download this file** saved it, and **Library → Downloads** played it after the news server was disconnected. Playback with a commercial provider or an external player still needs verification on a physical phone. Archive formats and limitations are shared with TV. [Supported Usenet formats](usenet-playback.md)

**Offline video.** Long-press a Home poster, the title on Details, or an episode and choose Download. To save a particular version, choose Play manually, select a source and tap Download this file. Open Library → Downloads to see transfers, play completed videos, check storage use or delete an individual file. The Downloads heading on Home opens the same screen. Saved files belong to the active profile on this device. Play and Next Episode check for a completed copy of the exact movie or episode before requesting provider streams. Play manually lists the download first and lets you select another version. Removing the file restores normal source selection. Android stores each completed file in its original video container under the app-specific Movies/ReSeq folder. Progressive video files and resolved HTTP video links are supported. Adaptive playlists and raw torrents require a downloadable file alternative.

**Google Drive.** In Settings, open Google Drive backup and restore and connect the same Google account as the TV. Connecting or reconnecting makes no backup and leaves automatic sync off on this device. Choose Restore to load your setup before making a backup. The apps share the Drive OAuth client and backup format. The standard backup includes IPTV logins, playlists, guide settings, Sports preferences, TVDB keys and orders, mixes and shuffle choices. Current builds also transfer supported TMDB, Gemini, debrid, MDBList and AnimeSkip keys for matching profiles. Profile-specific settings require matching profile IDs and names. Google sign-in is per device. For a different source profile choose Restore TV Mix and Shuffle, review its counts and confirm the destination. This merges saved mixes and series choices. Standard Restore uses no passphrase.

**Provider keys.** Back up keys saves the active profile’s TMDB, Gemini, debrid, MDBList and AnimeSkip keys in a separate passphrase-encrypted Drive file. On another device choose Restore keys and enter the same passphrase. Both builds need the provider-key controls. TV RC 3.1 can supply a standard backup, including IPTV, TVDB, TV Mix and Shuffle. It cannot export the separate provider-key file.

**Tracking and account sync.** Open Settings → Tracking and TVDB → MDBList to connect an account, choose its library lists or configure ratings. Open Settings → Nuvio account to link the app. After you approve in the browser, return to ReSeq. It checks the existing code immediately and starts the account sync when signed in. Check approval repeats the status check if needed.

**Trailer data use in development.** In **Settings → Home and title details**, turn off **Allow trailers on mobile data** to limit trailers to Wi-Fi or Ethernet on this device. The switch starts on to preserve existing playback. It covers title details, the trailer gallery, and manual or automatic Watch next previews. A change from Wi-Fi to cellular stops an active trailer.

**Episode history in development.** Tap an episode tile to play or resume it. Long-press it for the full synopsis, playback choices, downloads and watch history. Remove last watch shows the date of the identifiable viewing and uses ReSeq’s undo history. Remove partial watch clears that episode’s resume position. Mark as watched accepts the current time, a local date, or a date and time. A date alone uses the start of that day. Season and previous-episode actions use the selected episode order.

**Manual sources in development.** Play manually opens on All, with a matching download first. Tap an add-on tab to filter its results. The tabs stay above the scrolling files. Release names, filenames and descriptions wrap to show their full text. The in-player source chooser uses the same tabs and file cards.

**Without a connection.** Home can show saved Continue Watching cards and the active profile’s Downloads. Open the info icon beside a download on Home or in Library → Downloads to see its saved details and play it. A download keeps the title, episode identity and available description, dates, runtime and cover art. Opening an older download’s Details while online fills in available information. Add-on catalogue rows and full details for other titles can require a connection.

**Chromecast.** Open Play manually or the mobile player and tap Cast to choose a receiver. The Cast bar opens playback, track and source controls while browsing. Movie and episode progress follows the receiver clock and uses the selected ReSeq profile and episode order. IPTV channels, replay and IPTV VOD skip catalogue tracking. Seek is available for on-demand video and catch-up. Downloads and Usenet stay on the phone. Provider headers and live TS buffering use a receiver-specific connection to the phone. The receiver determines the codecs and selectable audio and subtitle tracks.

**Build availability.** Home → Reorder and Settings → Home and title details → Reorder Home rows open the shared catalogue order editor. Guide programme sheets have an icon grid with Find in Nuvio. Sports and Calendar share compact channel recording and tuning controls. [Public Alpha 12 downloads and release notes](https://github.com/abbaskip/Nuvio-ReSeq/releases/tag/mobile-v0.1.0-alpha.12)
