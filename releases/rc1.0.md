# Nuvio ReSeq RC1.0

## What’s new

- Hold **OK** on a channel in the Live TV guide or under **Watch on your channels** in Sports Hub, then choose **Open in multi view**. The selected station opens with sound in pane 1. Back returns focus to that station.
- Sports Hub keeps confirmed college basketball finals across a restart. An overdue game awaiting its first score shows **Status pending** and sorts with games that have already started.
- Home and large playlists load faster. On an onn 4K Pro, loading a 100,000-entry playlist took 5.2 seconds, down from 24.5 seconds on beta 10.12. Home reached fully drawn about 14% sooner on the same box.
- Guide reminders appear when due, including during playback. The guide clock and current programme advance while live TV plays. Stored v3 guides can upgrade in place.
- M3U playlists can supply movies and series to On Demand and the stream picker. Enable each playlist under **Settings → IPTV → On Demand**. Movie and series import is opt-in for existing and new M3Us. Allow about 85 MiB per 100,000 playlist-listed films.

## Updating from the beta

Open **Settings → About → Check for updates** to install RC1.0 over your signed ReSeq beta and keep your profiles, Continue Watching and IPTV settings. Android 12 and later also support idle installs when **Install updates automatically** and the TV’s install permission for ReSeq are enabled.

The installed build used 24.05 MiB more compiled storage than beta 10.12 on the onn. Physical Fire TV Stick qualification and live-account checks are still open. [Device results and remaining checks](https://github.com/abbaskip/Nuvio-ReSeq/blob/perf-device-qualification/docs/performance-integration-results.md).
