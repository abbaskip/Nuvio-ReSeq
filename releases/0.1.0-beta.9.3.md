# Nuvio ReSeq 0.1.0-beta.9.3

## Changes

- Live cricket now includes ESPN Test scores, including Pakistan v Sri Lanka, with multi-day match visibility and scores before both teams have batted. Full-day broadcasts are no longer mistaken for loop channels.
- Motorsport adds Supercars (including V8 Supercars aliases), Formula 2, Formula 3 and other TheSportsDB championships. Follow All Motorsport or individual competitions.
- Sports coverage now includes missing provider categories while preserving existing follows and category customizations. Lawn bowls, cornhole and ten pin bowling appear under More Sports and in TV Now independently of a provider fixture, with live labels when confirmed by guide metadata.
- Continue Watching preserves the shuffle icon and selected episode during metadata refreshes. Long-press Play manually resolves refreshed episode cards and opens source selection while retaining TVDB episode-order checks.
- Includes the withdrawn beta 9.2 improvements: Continue Watching history and loading behavior, profile-matched TVDB key sync through Drive, provider-ordered Xtream groups, and playback, Sports matching, stream-list and progress-saving optimizations. Personal TMDB credentials remain local to the profile.

## Validation and limitations

- Automated regression, build, lint-budget and packaged-service checks cover this combined release. No physical TV or live-account tests were performed.
- Sports listings and scores depend on provider availability; current guide airings without confirmed live metadata retain the ON AIR label.
