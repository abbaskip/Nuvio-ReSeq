# Nuvio ReSeq Mobile 0.1.0 Alpha 3

Install the universal APK over Mobile Alpha 2, or choose arm64-v8a for a smaller download on 64-bit ARM devices. Both require Android 7.0 or later. The mobile package installs alongside ReSeq TV.

- From Home, open catalogue rows and collection folders. From a title, browse the cast, filmography, related titles and collections. Library includes IPTV movies and series, recordings, media servers, cloud files and Watch Party.
- In the player, double-tap to seek, swipe horizontally to scrub, swipe vertically to adjust brightness or volume, or hold to play at double speed. The controls include audio, subtitles, chapters, episodes and source selection. TV Mix uses the shared ReSeq session to continue through episodes.
- Under Live TV, long-press a channel for its settings. Open the guide for catch-up and recording, or Multiview to tune more than one channel. Sports and Calendar include team management, fixture details and standings.
- Long-press a Home poster or a title or episode on Details to download automatically. To choose the file, use **Play manually → select a source → Download this file**. Play checks the active profile’s completed downloads first. File downloads require a source that returns a video file.
- Connect your Google account under **Settings → Google Drive backup and restore**, then choose **Restore**. Connecting makes no backup. For provider keys, use the same passphrase with **Back up keys** and **Restore keys** on builds that support them. The existing TV RC 3.1 can supply the standard IPTV and TVDB backup.

Coverage and outstanding device checks are tracked by screen. [Mobile feature list](https://github.com/abbaskip/Nuvio-ReSeq/blob/mobile-v0.1.0-alpha.3/docs/reseq-mobile.md)

Mobile layouts adapt homelessbrian’s NuvioPlusIPTV-Mobile, Nuvio Mobile and ghbarker’s phone port. The shared engine builds on Nuvio by Tapframe and contributors and Nuvio + IPTV.
